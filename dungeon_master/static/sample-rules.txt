ARCHIVE TEST RULES
Original demonstration material for testing the Rules library. This is not an official Dungeons & Dragons rulebook.

LANTERN WARD
A character carrying a lit ward lantern may spend one ember to protect an ally from a shadow creature. The ally ignores the next point of shadow damage. The ward lasts until the lantern goes out or the scene ends.

RESTING AT A WAYSHRINE
After a quiet hour at a wayshrine, each traveler regains two embers. A character can benefit from a particular wayshrine only once per day. A hostile creature entering the shrine interrupts the rest.

EXPLORING IN DARKNESS
Travelers without a light source must move slowly in the underground passages. A ward lantern illuminates nearby markings but does not reveal hidden creatures. Searching for a concealed door takes one exploration turn.
